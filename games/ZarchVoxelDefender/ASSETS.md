# Assets

**Art direction:** A crisp in-engine isometric view of a complete 10×10×10 voxel cube floating in a black brutalist interface. The lower three to four layers contain phosphor-green voxel terrain. A compact lime-and-cyan craft occupies the front-middle airspace, red-orange landers descend from the rear upper layers, white-cyan lasers cross the volume, and restrained amber impacts punctuate combat. Thin acid-green cube bounds and minimal cream HUD typography provide structure without filling the negative space.

| Asset | Role | Intended size | Source |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Zarch browser-game visual target | Defines camera, cube framing, palette, object scale, HUD hierarchy, and gameplay density. | 16:9 reference only | `/manus-storage/zarch-voxel-defender-reference_c1abc04e.png` |
| Phosphor terrain texture | Adds restrained mineral detail beneath the faceted filled heightfield; the luminous wireframe is generated from actual terrain triangles. | 2-voxel tile, repeated across the 10×10 surface | `/manus-storage/zarch-terrain-texture_c1c1046c.png` |

## Visual Target Prompt

The reference was generated as an actual 16:9 game-engine screenshot: a centered elevated orthographic 10×10×10 cube, low green terrain, lime/cyan craft, red-orange landers, cyan laser, orange impact, thin cube bounds, and a compact brutalist HUD. Fog, motion blur, lens flare, photorealism, and dense scenery outside the cube were explicitly excluded.
