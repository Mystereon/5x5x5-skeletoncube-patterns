# Pattern expansion 49–54

The next six patterns deliberately widen the library beyond particles, weather, and simple voxel objects. They use **negative space**, staged cinematic scenes, and familiar game/puzzle iconography so that the skeletal frame itself becomes part of the image.

| # | Pattern | Visual concept | Motion loop | Short actions |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 49 | **Black Hole Vortex** | A dark central void surrounded by curved violet, indigo, and cold-cyan accretion arcs. | Orbiting streams tighten, flash at the event horizon, then shed a faint outward lensing ripple. | Primary flips swirl direction; secondary raises or lowers the accretion energy. |
| 50 | **Stargate Dial-Up** | An illuminated ring hovering over a stone-textured base with seven amber chevrons. | Chevrons lock one-by-one, the ring fills with blue aqueous light, then the gate settles into a bright portal. | Primary restarts the dial sequence; secondary holds/releases the open portal. |
| 51 | **3-D Defender** | A cyan player ship rides the front plane while red/orange enemy formations circle through depth. | Enemies drift toward the viewer, the player fires a blue-white bolt, and occasional bursts mark a hit. | Primary fires immediately; secondary shifts the player ship. |
| 52 | **3-D Chequerboard** | Black void is as important as the lit tiles: a white/cyan chequer plane folds, shears, and rotates through the volume. | Flat board → diagonal staircase → inverted volumetric grid → flat board. | Primary reverses the transform; secondary changes the accent hue. |
| 53 | **Hellraiser Puzzle Cube** | A dark brass labyrinth cube, thin icy-blue seams, and amber-gold glyph pulses. | The lattice rotates in slow sections; seams flare, a void opens, and the cube mechanically closes again. | Primary forces an opening phase; secondary adjusts glyph brightness. |
| 54 | **3-D Rubik’s Cube** | Six familiar face colours mapped to an inset 3×3×3 cube, separated by black grid gaps. | A face turns, the colours scramble, several turns follow, then the cube pauses in a solved state. | Primary forces the next layer twist; secondary forces a solved reveal. |

Each implementation remains physically readable at five voxels per edge. High-frequency detail is carried by motion and colour contrast rather than trying to render a literal fine mesh that would collapse on a 5×5×5 frame.
